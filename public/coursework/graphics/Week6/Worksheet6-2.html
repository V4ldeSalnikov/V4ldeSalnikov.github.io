Nearest filtering selects the closest texel, giving crisp results when magnified but causing obvious aliasing when the texture shrinks. Linear filtering blends neighboring texels, which softens magnification and reduces stair-stepping during minification. Mipmaps pre-filter the texture at successively lower resolutions so minification can use a level that matches the on-screen size, significantly reducing shimmering. Disabling mipmaps forces the renderer to sample the full-resolution image even when the geometry is far away, making aliasing easier to spot.
