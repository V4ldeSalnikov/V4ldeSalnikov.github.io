## Math

Inline math like $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ renders via KaTeX at build time. Block math, too:

$$
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}
$$

The rendering equation, for reference:

$$
L_o(\mathbf{p}, \omega_o) = L_e(\mathbf{p}, \omega_o)
  + \int_\Omega f_r(\mathbf{p}, \omega_i, \omega_o)\, L_i(\mathbf{p}, \omega_i)\, \cos\theta_i \, d\omega_i
$$
