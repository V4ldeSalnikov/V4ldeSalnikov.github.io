Ongoing research at [Aarhus University](https://international.au.dk/) on
post-training **Danish foundation models**, with a focus on RLAIF (RL from AI
feedback) variants adapted to low-resource languages.

## The constraint

The bottleneck for Danish post-training isn't compute — it's **coverage and
quality of preference and instruction data**. Standard recipes assume an
abundant pool of human preference judgements and instruction-tuning conversations.
For Danish, both are scarce, and naive English→Danish translation introduces
its own distributional artifacts.

## What I work on

- **RLAIF pipelines** that produce useful preference signal at Danish-scale
  data, including adjustments to the judge prompt and pairwise sampling to
  reduce English-bias leak-through.
- **Synthetic instruction-tuning datasets** for Danish, built to fit the same
  chat-`messages` schema used by my [open-source Scandinavian SFT release](/projects/scandinavian-instruction-tuning/).
  Reusing one schema makes ablations across post-training stages much easier.

This page will track outputs as they appear — preprints, datasets, and
write-ups on what worked and what didn't.
