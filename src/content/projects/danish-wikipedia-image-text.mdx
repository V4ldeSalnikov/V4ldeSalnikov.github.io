The first large-scale **Danish image–text dataset** that keeps neighboring article
context, not just a single caption.

## The gap this fills

Most Wikipedia-derived image–text datasets reduce each image to caption + alt-text,
dropping the surrounding paragraph that usually disambiguates what the picture is
actually showing. For English, the loss is recoverable from the sheer volume of
data. For Danish — where total available image–text pairs are orders of magnitude
smaller — every bit of context you throw away hurts downstream models.

## What's preserved

For each image extracted from Danish Wikipedia, the dataset stores:

- The image URL and basic metadata.
- The literal caption from the figure.
- The **section heading** the image appears under.
- The **neighboring paragraph(s)** surrounding the image, so models can learn
  from longer-form image–text relationships rather than caption-only pairs.

This makes it usable for tasks where the caption alone is ambiguous: a portrait
captioned only with a name needs the surrounding article to know whether that
person was a 17th-century theologian or a 20th-century footballer.
